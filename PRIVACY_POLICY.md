# SkillSwap — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026

SkillSwap ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Data We Collect
- **Account data**: name, email address, college/institution, and password (hashed).
- **Profile data**: department, year of study, bio, avatar, cover photo, skills, portfolio links, and uploaded resume (PDF/DOCX/TXT).
- **Activity data**: connections, messages, scheduled sessions, ratings, badges, certificates, and leaderboard points.
- **Technical data**: IP address, device/browser information, and usage logs.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To create and manage your account.
- To match you with peers and generate AI-powered recommendations and compatibility scores.
- To enable chat, sessions, notifications, and the leaderboard.
- To operate and improve the platform, and to ensure security.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
- Data is stored in **MongoDB 7**.
- Passwords are encrypted using strong one-way hashing (bcrypt) and never stored in plaintext.
- Access is protected by JWT-based authentication and role-based authorization (user/admin).
- We apply reasonable safeguards; however, no system is completely secure.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party providers for:
- Email delivery (verification codes, password resets, notifications).
- Hosting and infrastructure (e.g., Railway/Render, Vercel/Netlify).
- The AI service, which processes skills and goals for recommendations (no sale of data).

These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service.

## 5. Cookies & Local Storage
We use cookies and browser local storage to maintain your session, remember preferences, and keep you logged in. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function correctly.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Modify** or update your data via your profile settings.
- **Delete** your account and associated personal data (subject to legal retention).
- **Object** to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

## 7. Data Retention
We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Anonymized activity records may be retained for analytics.

## 8. Children's Privacy
SkillSwap is intended for college students and is not directed to users under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region).

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the platform or email.

## 10. Contact
For privacy questions or requests, contact: **privacy@skillswap.example**
